# Market

Several members attempt the same task, and a referee picks among their
results. The referee is a rule signed before the work starts: a test
suite, a holdout set, or a pinned judge. It has no opinion of its own.

Works well for: checkable work at width.

## Run it

1. Bind the referee first. Name it, and say what a winning result must
   pass. No referee, no market: without one, pick another pattern.
2. Give every member the same brief and the same budget. Let them work
   apart; different approaches are the point.
3. Run the referee on every result that comes back. The best score wins.
   Ties go to the cheaper result.
4. Report the winner, the referee's scores for every entrant, and the
   spend. Losing results are archived, not deleted.

## Keep it honest

- Never change the referee after the work starts. A referee chosen after
  seeing the results is an opinion.
- Width costs budget. Fit the number of entrants to the budget, and say
  what a wider market would buy.
- The decider on the receipt is the referee.
