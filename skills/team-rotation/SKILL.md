# Rotation

The members take turns in each role: proposer, critic, reframer. Each turn
moves every role one seat on. The leader holds the patch and decides.

Works well for: reframing, negotiation and open questions. It starts
untested, so prefer another pattern until receipts show it pays here.

## Run it

1. Name the roles and the order. Three roles and three members is enough.
2. Each turn, the proposer offers a framing, the critic attacks it, and
   the reframer restates the problem from another side.
3. Rotate after every turn, so no member keeps one stance.
4. Stop when a turn brings no new framing, or the lease nears its end. The
   leader then names the framings worth keeping. Several answers is the
   diverge setting; pick one when the task needs one.

## Keep it honest

- Rotation spends turns. Cap the turns to the budget before starting.
- A framing is kept for what it shows, not for who proposed it.
- The decider on the receipt is the leader.
