## Decisions and canon corrections

- `Host` lives under the vault-contract `host` module. No ctl wire shape or
  `CONTRACT_VERSION` changes. Sandbox and edge runtimes inject capabilities;
  no process-wide singleton or ambient secret lookup is introduced.
- Control-key storage uses actual HMAC-SHA256, not a bare hash or the existing
  BLAKE3 bearer-token MAC. LMDB writer serialization enforces unique digests.
- Real tenant checks require kernel fscrypt evidence plus the effective UID of
  `oneiron-<vault-name>` and an owner-only vault directory. Non-Linux probes fail closed.
- CROSS-ARCH-0022's previously deferred ctl shed and reply-resume seams now have
  engine callers. The private supervisor's PSI loop is not in this repository;
  its shared ordering helper is shipped here, not an invented fleet controller.
