## Storage and canon decisions

- OF-296's runtime B5 pointer is stale: the referenced runtime page has no B5 section. The registry's tier and RESTORE laws define this implementation.
- Mixed databases require row-family classification. Canonical text/phonetic caller instructions are retained as rebuild witnesses; an older index without its witness is refused rather than restored as silently empty search.
- `commitment_due:v1:` primary rows encode optional scheduling enrollment, not merely a derived index. Stored-but-unindexed commitment series are legal. Primary obligations stay canonical; reverse/project sidecars rebuild. The skill-attribution processed cursor likewise cannot be guessed from the maximum evidence sequence, which may be unprocessed.
- Meter events are canonical stamped facts. Rollups are derived and omitted from checkpoints; the ledger rebuilds them under one write snapshot without re-pricing or double-counting. Cached limits are runtime state and disappear on restore.
- CROSS-ARCH-0019 should spell out the fscrypt and root-owned UID-allocation probe requirements. Probe UAPI layout was checked against Linux `fscrypt.h`; the ioctl request deliberately encodes a 9-byte flexible prefix, while the provided buffer accommodates the full policy.
- The GPU ticket's `runtime.rs` is now `runtime/`. The engine registry is the regional single-writer state machine; deployment, probes and lease-renewal scheduling belong to its host.

- Device `ls:` rows are canonical authority, not process/runtime leases. Their revoked-pubkey floor survives restore and still rejects the old key under any same-vault client id.
- The detector default is 100 writes, within its 1,024-observation window. Manifest configuration still sets the bound. Proposal pressure is separate at 100,000 submissions and never refuses a proposal. The fixture crosses the actual production threshold.
- Tripwire observation markers use a distinct namespace from checker outcome reasons. The checker assertions remain intact; only their interpretation of additional observation metadata changed.
