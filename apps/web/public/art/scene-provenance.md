# Optional Recall scenes

These original, AI-generated landscapes are not photographs or product screenshots.
Generated 2026-09-19 with requested model `gpt-image-2`, quality `high`, via the
CPA Image API. Night editions were edited from the refined day compositions.
The provider returned 1672×941 PNGs despite the requested 2304×1296 size.
Exports were downsampled to 1600×900 and encoded as WebP; no upscaling or
additional tonal treatment was applied. These copies are byte-identical exports.

The texture is generated irregular screen-print / photogravure grain, **not
verified canonical 8×8 Bayer dither**. The pairs are matched scenes, not exact
pixel-identical toggle plates. They are not seamless or mobile/4K masters.
Existing Fable plates, fields, flock, faces and marks remain unchanged.
Rain garden is not installed.

Placement: opt-in Recall pre-question backdrop only. None is the default.
App theme selects the authored day/night edition. Text always sits on the
opaque existing sheet token. No uploads, animation, filtering or new grain.
