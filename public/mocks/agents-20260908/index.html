The agents room, six ways

The Hokuei day, told the same way on every page. Night is the default. Compare by feel; every page carries the same roster, composer and ground line as the app.

m1The rail
Eiri’s room with the work rail open: runs unfolded to their helpers, the side conversations, the fold. Head as one line, your turns indented. The canonical composition. m2The edge
The same room with the rail folded to a hairline and its rotated count, no head at all, your turns in mono. Both alternatives in one image. m3The house
Everyone and you. A face and a name once per change of speaker, Antevon’s handoff lines, the rail grouped by agent. m4A run opened
The COO intro as its own thread: the way back, the title line, the helpers nested under Eiri’s words, the gate with the inbox’s verbs, the open bar in the rail. m5The panel
The floating chat over the vault, 420 wide. The rail folds into run cards above the composer; the room act leads to the page. m6Two inks
A free composition with one risk: the voice is the ink, and the runs print where they were asked for. Three mono lines at the foot explain it.
